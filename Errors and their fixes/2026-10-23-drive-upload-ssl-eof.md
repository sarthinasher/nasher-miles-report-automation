# Drive upload fails with "EOF occurred in violation of protocol (_ssl.c:2437)"; the date is lost or every location is exported again

- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Reported as:** "why is there a gap in 12th to 15th October in Full Flow FMS please check and fix that". Part of the answer: Sales runs 39792593177 and 39751069123 (22-Oct) failed on the upload, `Drive upload error: EOF occurred in violation of protocol (_ssl.c:2437)` for 20.10.2026, and the 13-20/10 back-fill lost 15.10 / 16.10 the same way.
- **Symptom:**
  - `[9] Uploading to Google Drive...` followed ~2 ms later by `[!] Drive upload error: EOF occurred in violation of protocol (_ssl.c:2437)` and `[✗] Drive upload failed. Check that the folder is shared with the service account's client_email.` (the hint is wrong: sharing was fine).
  - Seen in Sales 39716075711 (15.10, 16.10, 18.10, 19.10), 39613882104 (17.10 twice, 20.10), 39751069123 (21.10), 39792593177 (20.10 twice -> `20.10.2026: FAILED after 2 attempt(s)`), 39572252190 (2x), 39396241597 (7x); Pick Pack 39394101857 (19.10); Order Enquiry 39618329909 (20.10).
  - The date's retry exported every location again: Pick Pack 19/10 took 29 min instead of 14 (34 exports), Sales 18/10 and 19/10 took 70 min each (34 exports).
- **Where:** `common/drive_utils.upload_to_drive` (used by every report script). The Drive client is built once at the start of the run (`common/daily_export.main` -> `get_drive_service()`) and first used again for the upload, 30-35 min later (after the browser export).
- **Root cause:** the first Drive call of the upload (the `files().list` lookup) fails on the connection itself - within ~2 ms, before any reply - with an SSL EOF: the client's long-idle HTTPS connection to Google had been dropped. `upload_to_drive` retried only `HttpError` 5xx; any other exception printed `Drive upload error` and returned None at once, so one dropped connection failed the date. The date's second attempt reused the same client and could fail the same way (20.10 in 39792593177 failed twice). Then `remove_files` in each script's `finally` deleted the per-location files, so the "reuse finished locations on retry" logic (`_location_files`) found nothing to reuse and exported every location again.
- **Why it wasn't caught:** before 19-Oct a run took 2-3 min, so the client was never idle long enough. The message blamed folder sharing; the retries hid the cost (a re-export), and when the re-export happened to upload fine the run stayed green.
- **Fix:**
  - `common/drive_utils.py`:
    - `upload_to_drive`: resumable `MediaFileUpload` with `chunksize = 8 MB` (multiple of 256 KB), driven with `next_chunk()` in a loop; the whole upload (lookup + create/update) runs through `with_drive_retries`: up to 6 attempts, waits 2, 4, 8, 16, 30 s + 0-1 s jitter (about 1 min of waiting), a NEW Drive client (new connection pool) before each retry. The lookup runs again on each try, so a create that reached Drive before the connection broke is updated, not duplicated. Logs `[!] Drive upload of <name>: SSLEOFError: ... (transient) - attempt 1/6 failed, retrying in 2.4s on a new connection...`; the final error now shows its type.
    - `is_transient_drive_error`: retries `ssl.SSLError` (incl. `SSLEOFError`), `ConnectionError` (reset / aborted / `BrokenPipeError`, `RemoteDisconnected`), `TimeoutError` / `socket.timeout`, `http.client.HTTPException` (`IncompleteRead`...), `httplib2.ServerNotFoundError`, messages like "EOF occurred in violation of protocol", and `HttpError` 429 / 500 / 502 / 503 / 504 / 403 `rateLimitExceeded` / `userRateLimitExceeded`. 400 / 401 / 404 / other 403s are not retried.
    - `get_drive_service`: `httplib2.Http(timeout=120)` so a dead socket raises (and is retried) instead of hanging until the workflow timeout; `cache_discovery=False`.
    - `list_file_names` (catch-up listing at the start) uses the same retries.
  - `common/report_merge.remove_attempt_files` + Pick Pack / Sales / Order Enquiry: after a failed UPLOAD the per-location files are kept, so the date's retry reuses them and only uploads again. After any other failure they are deleted as before (a bad export is never reused).
  - Tests: `tests/test_drive_utils.py` (SSL EOF on a chunk then success on a rebuilt client, SSL EOF on the lookup then update, give up after N attempts with growing waits, 400/401/404 not retried, 403 permission vs. rate limit, transient / non-transient classification, listing retry, files kept after an upload failure).
- **Evidence:** 39394101857 `2026-10-20T08:47:08.4853887Z [9] Uploading to Google Drive...` / `08:47:08.4874968Z [!] Drive upload error: EOF occurred in violation of protocol (_ssl.c:2437)`; 39792593177 `14:40:36.5044304Z` and `15:14:44.7309609Z [!] Drive upload error: EOF occurred in violation of protocol (_ssl.c:2437)`, summary `20.10.2026: FAILED after 2 attempt(s): Drive upload failed.`; 39751069123 `10:30:52.4233438Z` same for 21.10.
- **Blast radius:** Sales 20.10.2026 (not uploaded by 39792593177) and other Sales dates of 13-21/10 that failed this way and were not re-done; Pick Pack / Order Enquiry dates where the retry also failed. Every hit cost a full re-export of all locations, holding the `vinculum-session` group longer.
